# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, residential and commercial, evaluating RH Studio for an architecture project. They land here researching or comparing studios, often on mobile or a laptop in a quiet moment, not mid-task. The job to be done: assess credibility and taste quickly, then reach out.

## Product Purpose

RH-LANDING is the marketing site for RH Studio, an architecture practice led by Rafael Haryanto, IAI, founded 2014. It exists to build trust through the quality of its own craft (the site is a portfolio piece in itself) and to convert visitor interest into inquiries. Success looks like a visitor coming away with a clear sense of the studio's point of view and taking the next step toward contact.

## Brand Personality

Refined, warm, confident. Voice is understated, not promotional, letting the work and the typography carry weight rather than marketing copy. Bilingual (Indonesian/English) editorial tone: serif display type for statements, monospace for labels and metadata, generous whitespace, warm cream-and-terracotta palette rather than cool corporate tones.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/startup templates: no hero-metric blocks, no gradient text, no identical icon-card grids, no glassmorphism.
- Corporate/stiff B2B architecture firm sites: avoid stock-photo-heavy, overly formal, sterile presentation. The studio's confidence should come through restraint and craft, not credentialing badges or dense paragraphs of institutional copy.

## Design Principles

- Let the work speak: photography and typography carry the argument, copy stays sparse and precise.
- Editorial rhythm over template grid: vary spacing and composition section to section rather than repeating a card pattern.
- Warmth through material color, not decoration: the cream/terracotta palette and serif display type are the brand, not an accent layer on top of a neutral system.
- Bilingual parity: every section must read naturally in both Indonesian and English, no string should be designed around one language's length.
- Motion as confidence, not spectacle: reveals and transitions should feel deliberate and unhurried, never bouncy or attention-grabbing for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all scroll reveals and transitions (already implemented in src/index.css). Maintain sufficient contrast between ink/cream and accent/background pairs in both light and dark mode tokens.
